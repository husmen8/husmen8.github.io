Projects

bozkır: captured ground, placed by terrain

2026 · solo project

A few square metres of real ground, scanned from drone photos as 3D Gaussian splats, made into seamless tiles and laid over kilometres of generated terrain. The land decides where each material goes; rivers are cut in metres and filled with water measured from the same photos. Everything runs in the browser.

project page · live demo · video · code

Gaussian splatting SLAM on a Jetson Orin Nano

graduation project · experiment

With little hardware and budget, the question was whether an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano could run Gaussian splatting SLAM live, mapping as the camera moves rather than from a pre-made 3D map. Based on Gaussian-SLAM (Yugay et al.), adapted to CUDA 12.2 and the Jetson's memory. Path finding and SLAM on ROS 2 were the planned next step.
